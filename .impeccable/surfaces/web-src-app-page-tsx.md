# Home (and site-wide world): Dyt. Elif Sude Özkan

Scope: home page `/`, and the visual world every route inherits. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: prospective clients in Nilüfer/Bursa and online, mostly on phones, arriving from Instagram or search.
Action: write on WhatsApp, or send an appointment request (an email; no booking).
Proof/content: verified CV facts, real services from Sanity, her own photographs once uploaded. No testimonials, numbers or stock food.
Constraints: brief-pinned palette (forest green, olive, warm off-white, cream, ink, bronze; blue only for the route bar), Turkish glyphs, photos via Sanity, designed empty states.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every photograph and fact is mounted like a herbarium specimen: a cream sheet on a deep-green genus folder, held by linen tape, beside a printed label carrying verified data. It refuses the category default of headline left, smiling portrait right, a row of service cards below.

OWN-WORLD: Deep forest green #204B38 and dark olive #2C321F own whole regions (committed strategy, green near 40% of the page). Cream sheets #F6F3EC / #FFFDF8 sit on them as flat plates; linen tape strips hold photos; labels are thin ink rules with small sans text and tabular figures. Display is a high-contrast didone; text is a calm grotesk. Depth comes only from flat overlap, never shadows or gradients (raise from Łowicz papercut). Sections change by hard full-width colour cuts (raise from the Bebop title card).

STORY: The visitor meets her photograph, reads who she is from the label, understands the approach (evidence-based, individual, sustainable), sees the real services, then writes on WhatsApp or requests an appointment.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed green folder. Left of an off-centre mounting rail (raise from the emission-line rail): the one-line approach set large in the didone, then the two actions, WhatsApp primary, appointment request secondary. Right of the rail: a tall cream sheet with the portrait taped on (or its designed empty state) and the specimen label at its foot: name, Diyetisyen, Acıbadem Üniversitesi Beslenme ve Diyetetik 2025, Nilüfer/Bursa, yüz yüze ve online. Phone: headline and actions first, the sheet directly below, both inside the first two screens.

FORM: Herbarium sheet, position 5 of my ordered list of 7; seed:cc7af9f6ddd7b450. Signature interaction: the thin blue route-progress line on internal navigation; in the gallery, the opened sheet comes forward and the rest dim one step (raise from the particle-detector display). Motion grammar: sheets settle in with a short translate and fade, no bounce; navigation state is drawn as rule weight, not colour.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
